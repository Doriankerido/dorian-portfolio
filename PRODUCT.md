# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers and design team leads. They look through the portfolio to decide whether to invite Dorian to interview for a UX Designer role. They scan quickly, so each case study has to show the problem, the decisions and the reasoning within a few screens.

## Product Purpose

A personal UX design portfolio for Dorian Kerido, a UX designer based in Tel Aviv, Israel, who completed UX design studies in 2026 and is available for new projects. Success is an invitation to interview.

## Positioning

Dorian turns complex systems into experiences that feel effortless. The work is operational tooling: scheduling platforms and zero-to-one products, where clarity under real data is the design problem.

## Operating Context

Case studies are written from design work on Skid, a food photography shoot scheduling platform. The work involved Wolt-listed restaurants, photographers and coordination managers. Prototypes were built with Figma and Claude AI.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: Home, Work, Contact, and the Skid case study at `/work/skid-planner`.
- Stack: React 18, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS v3, Framer Motion, React Router v6.
- All site copy is in English only, written with plain hyphens and no em dashes.
- Case study images are screenshots of prototypes; live prototype lives at a claude.ai artifact link.

## Brand Commitments

- Name shown as "Dorian Kerido" in the nav, set in DM Mono capitals like the nav links.
- Existing type system: Tanker (display), Satoshi (body), DM Mono (labels).
- Warm cream background with a saturated blue ink and orange accent (existing implementation, not newly decided here).

## Evidence on Hand

- Skid case study (`/work/skid-planner`) with five screenshots captured from the live prototype (`public/images/skid-planner/`). Research: 4 interviews. No user testing or result metrics yet; the page carries TODO placeholders for those.
- Contact details on the Contact page, LinkedIn on the footer.
- Absent: measured outcome metrics. Do not invent result numbers; use only facts from the projects.
- Behance link in the footer is still a placeholder.

## Product Principles

- Show the reasoning, not just the screens: every decision gets a "why".
- Complexity made simple is the through-line of the work and of the pages themselves.
- Never fabricate metrics, testimonials or client claims.
- Scannable first: a hiring manager should get the story from headings and numbers alone.
- English only, plain punctuation.
